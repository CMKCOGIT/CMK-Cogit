Services and timelines

What is the difference between a ready-made template and a custom build?

No ready-made template the structure and design are already built: you send copy, images, logo and information and we apply them to the template. It is faster and cheaper. With a custom, structure, design and features are designed for your business, and the scope is validated by the team before payment.

How soon will my website be ready?

For ready-made templates: landing page in 3 to 5 business days, portfolio in 3 to 5 and company website in 3 to 7. For custom builds, the timeline is set in the scope. The timeline counts from payment confirmation and receipt of your materials, and you follow each step in platform.

Can I ask for changes to the website?

Yes. With a ready-made template we adjust information and content within the template's structure; changes to structure or design count as customization and may cost extra. With a custom build, revisions follow what was agreed during validation.

What do I need to send to get started?

Copy, logo, images and contact information. The platform shows a list of pending items and you send the files right there.

Domain and code

Is the domain mine?

Yes. The site is published on your domain (e.g. yourcompany.com), registered in your name; domain registration and renewal costs are yours.

Is the website's source code mine?

For custom builds, yes: once paid in full, the website and source code are yours. With a ready-made template, the template's code belongs to CMKCOGIT and is not handed over; you use the published site and remain the owner of your copy, images and brand.

Payment

How do I pay?

On the platform, with Pix, credit card or boleto, in Mercado Pago's secure environment. Fixed-price items go straight to payment; custom projects open for payment after the team validates them.

Why do you ask for a CPF or CNPJ?

To identify who is hiring, issue the contract and invoice and process the payment. Only you and the team see this data, and it appears masked on screen. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel my purchase?

Yes. Within 7 days of payment the refund is full. After that, if development has already started, the refund is proportional to what has not yet been done. Details in the Terms of Use.

Support and platform

Is there support after delivery?

Every project includes 1 month of free support for fixes and small adjustments. After that, you can choose Essential Technical Support (on demand, from R$ 150 per request) or Ongoing Technical Support (a flat R$ 150/month) in Build your solution.

What is "Minha área" (My account)?

It is the client platform, at app.cmkcogit.com.br. That is where your requests, payments, the progress of each project, files and notifications live.

I forgot my password. What now?

On the platform sign-in screen, click "Esqueci minha senha" (Forgot my password) and we will email you a link. If you signed in with Google, use the same "Continuar com Google" button.
