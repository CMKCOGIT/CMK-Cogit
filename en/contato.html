GENERAL QUESTIONS

Maybe the answer you're missing is here.

We have gathered the most common questions before a first conversation.

My question is something else
Do I need to know exactly which solution I want?

No. You can come with just a problem, an idea or a goal. COGIT helps understand the situation and structure the right solution before development.

What is the difference between this page and “Build your solution”?

Here the focus is on talking directly and answering questions. In the configurator, you combine services, goals and features to get a more structured initial estimate.

Does COGIT work with small businesses and freelancers?

Yes. The solution is sized to each business's moment, priority and reality — from an initial digital presence to more complex platforms and systems.

How does the first conversation work?

We start by understanding the problem, the context and the expected result. Then we point out the most sensible next step, with no obligation to hire.

How much does a project cost and how long does it take?

Price and timeline depend on scope and complexity. You can check starting ranges in Solutions & pricing or put together an estimate in the configurator.

Does COGIT also evolve existing projects?

Yes. We can assess an existing operation, website, automation, system or digital product and structure improvements to experience, performance and technical evolution.
