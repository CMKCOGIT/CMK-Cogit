Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

CMKCOGIT values the privacy and protection of the personal data of its visitors, clients and partners.

This Policy explains, in plain terms, what data we collect on the website cmkcogit.com.br and on the client platform app.cmkcogit.com.br, what we use it for, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 — LGPD).

CMKCOGIT sells websites and digital solutions in two formats: ready-made templates, with a structure already built into which we insert the client's information, and custom projects, developed according to each client's needs.

3. Data Collected

We collect only the data needed to serve you and provide the services hired.

Data you give us

  • Name and, if provided, company name;
  • Email and phone/WhatsApp;
  • Platform password (stored encrypted; the CMKCOGIT team has no access to it);
  • CPF (individual) or CNPJ (company), to identify the contracting party, contract, invoice and payment;
  • Messages sent through the contact form, WhatsApp or email.

Project and purchase data

  • Solution put together on the website (template, chosen features and notes);
  • Requests, orders, amounts and receipts;
  • Content and files sent for the client's website (copy, logo, images, documents), up to 50 MB per file;
  • Project information, such as stages, deadlines, domain and service history;
  • Status and identification of payments made through Mercado Pago.

Data collected automatically

  • Platform access logs (date, time and IP address), required by the Brazilian Civil Rights Framework for the Internet (Law No. 12,965/2014);
  • Technical browser and device information, used for security and bug fixing;
  • Website browsing statistics (pages visited and interactions), only if you accept the optional analytics cookies.

Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with your Google account, we receive only your name, email and profile photo, as authorized by you on Google's screen. We do not have access to your Google password.

Data we do not collect

We do not receive or store your credit card details. Payment is made directly in Mercado Pago's secure environment.

4. How We Use Data

Service delivery

  • Create and maintain your account on the platform;
  • Record the chosen solution, validate the scope and calculate the price;
  • Develop, customize and publish your website or system;
  • Process payments and issue receipts.

Communication

  • Respond to contacts and requests;
  • Send updates about project progress, pending items and payments, by email, WhatsApp or on the platform;
  • Communicate important changes to the services or this Policy.

Improvement and security

  • Understand how the site is used to improve content and features (only with consent);
  • Fix technical faults;
  • Prevent fraud, unauthorized access and suspicious activity.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising and we do not sell personal data.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the grounds set out in art. 7 of the LGPD:

  • Performance of a contract and preliminary procedures: registration, quote, project development, payment and support;
  • Compliance with a legal obligation: retention of access logs, tax and accounting data;
  • Legitimate interest: platform security, fraud prevention and service improvement, always respecting your rights;
  • Consent: analytics cookies and non-essential communications, which may be withdrawn at any time;
  • Regular exercise of rights: defense in judicial, administrative or arbitration proceedings.

6. Data Sharing

We share data only with providers necessary for the services to work, who process the data according to our instructions and with appropriate security measures:

  • Supabase: database, user authentication and storage of files sent to the platform;
  • Vercel: hosting of the website and platform, and domain management;
  • Mercado Pago: payment processing by Pix, card and boleto;
  • Google: sign-in with a Google account (optional) and Google Analytics (only with your consent);
  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you choose to talk to us through that channel;
  • Public authorities: when there is a legal obligation or court order.

The websites and systems we build for our clients may process data about visitors to those sites. In these cases, the client is the controller of that data and CMKCOGIT acts as the processor, following the client's instructions.

7. International Transfer

Some providers (such as Supabase, Vercel and Google) may store or process data on servers located outside Brazil. In these cases, the transfer takes place under art. 33 of the LGPD, with providers that adopt data protection standards compatible with Brazilian law.

8. Storage and Retention Period

Data is stored in a protected environment and kept only for as long as necessary:

  • Account data: while the account is active or there is a project in progress;
  • Payment and tax data: for 5 years, as required by tax law;
  • Access logs: for at least 6 months, as required by the Brazilian Civil Rights Framework for the Internet;
  • Project files and content: during the project and the support period, and may be deleted at the client's request;
  • Contact messages without a purchase: for up to 12 months.

After these periods, data is deleted or anonymized, except when retention is required to comply with a legal obligation or defend rights.

9. Information Security

CMKCOGIT takes technical and administrative measures to protect data, such as:

  • Encrypted connection (HTTPS) on every page;
  • Passwords stored with encryption;
  • Per-user access control: each client sees only their own data and projects;
  • File storage in a private location, accessible only to the client and the responsible team;
  • Payments checked directly with Mercado Pago;
  • Infrastructure backups and monitoring;
  • Restricted internal access for the people who need the data to do their job.

No system is completely immune to risk. If a security incident occurs that could cause significant harm, we will notify the affected data subjects and Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), as required by the LGPD.

10. Cookies and Browser Storage

We use cookies and the browser's local storage in the following ways:

  • Necessary: keep you signed in to the platform, remember your cookie choice and temporarily store the solution you put together on the website until you sign in to the platform. They cannot be turned off, as the service does not work without them;
  • Analytics (optional): Google Analytics, to understand how the site is used. Only turned on if you accept, and can be turned off at any time.

We do not use advertising cookies. You can change your choice under or in your browser settings.

11. Data Subject Rights

Under art. 18 of the LGPD, you may request at any time:

  • Confirmation that we process your data;
  • Access to your data;
  • Correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • Anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed in breach of the law;
  • Portability of data to another provider;
  • Deletion of data processed based on consent;
  • Information about who we share your data with;
  • Withdrawal of consent;
  • Review of decisions made solely by automated means.

Some of your data (name, WhatsApp, email and password) can be changed directly in My profile, on the platform.

12. How to Make a Request

To exercise your rights, contact us at contato@cmkcogit.com.br, preferably with the subject “Privacy”, using the email registered on the platform.

We may ask for information to confirm your identity before fulfilling the request. We reply within 15 days.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you can also contact Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) through its website gov.br/anpd.

13. Children's Data

CMKCOGIT's services are intended for people over 18 or for companies. We do not knowingly collect data from children and adolescents. If this happens without the authorization of their legal guardians, the data will be deleted as soon as it is identified.

15. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated to reflect legal changes, new features or new providers. The date of the last update is always at the top of the page. Relevant changes will be communicated through the platform or by email.

16. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Policy is governed by Brazilian law, especially the General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018), the Brazilian Civil Rights Framework for the Internet (Law No. 12,965/2014) and the Consumer Protection Code (Law No. 8,078/1990).

The courts of the district of São José do Rio Preto/SP, Brazil, are elected as the forum, without prejudice to the consumer's right to file a claim in the courts of their place of residence.
