OUR MANIFESTO

Technology doesn't start with code. It starts with understanding the problem.

A project only creates value when it solves the right need. That's why, before talking about tools, we understand the routine, the bottlenecks and the goals of the people on the other side.

From a first digital presence to a system, an automation or a SaaS product, every decision needs a clear reason and must contribute to a real result.

In developmentOwn project · Sustainability and circular economy

Nexus Technology connecting waste to new opportunities.

Nexus was born from an idea created within COGIT and refined in partnership with one of the partners. The project is being structured to operate as its own business in the future and expand our commitment to impact solutions.

The goal is to bring companies that generate waste closer to organizations able to buy it or dispose of it correctly, bringing negotiation, traceability and certifications together in a digital environment.

For businesses
List, negotiate and prove the correct destination of waste.
For individuals free
Know where to dispose of items such as batteries, at no cost.

How it will work

  1. Those who generate it listCompanies or individuals list the materials they need to dispose of.
  2. Nexus connectsOrganizes the match-up, the negotiation and the record of each step.
  3. Those who reuse it receiveBuyers and recyclers find material, with traceability and certification.
A first step toward a future sustainable-projects arm of COGIT. Talk about an impact idea
