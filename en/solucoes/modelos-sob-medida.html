What we build specifically for your project

In custom projects, nothing is limited by an existing structure. Everything is designed from scratch for your context.

Navigation Structure

Pages, sections and navigation flows defined according to your business goal and your audience's behavior.

Exclusive Design

Specific Features

Integrations

PROCESS

How a custom project happens

A structured path to understand your context before building any solution.

01

Understanding the business

We talk about your context, audience, goals and what already exists. The more we understand, the better what we build.

02

Proposal and alignment

We present the technical approach, scope, schedule and price. Nothing moves forward without your approval and complete clarity.

03

Design and development

Our team develops the visual and technical structure, with check-ins along the way to ensure continuous alignment.

04

Review, testing and delivery

You review, we adjust as agreed, run the technical tests and publish your project with every detail checked.

Common questions about custom projects

Understand how projects built specifically for your business work.

With Ready-made Templates, we start from an existing structure built by COGIT, which we adapt with your brand identity. With Custom projects, structure, design and experience are created from scratch — specifically for your business, without the limits of a pre-existing template.

The timeline varies with the complexity and scope of the project. We set the schedule clearly in the proposal phase — before any development. We don't start until you know exactly when you will receive each deliverable.

The price is set based on the project's scope: number of pages, features, integrations, design complexity and timeline. We present the detailed proposal before any commitment on your part.

You don't need to arrive with everything defined. Our team leads the discovery process and helps structure what your project needs. The more you can share about your business, your clients and your goals, the better what we deliver.
